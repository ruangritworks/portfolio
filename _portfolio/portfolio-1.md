**Zanroo Thailand · Data Analyst · March–October 2025**

## Context

Client brands needed to understand consumer sentiment, brand perception, relevant trends, and competitor performance through social media data.

## My responsibilities

- Defined monitoring requirements based on client needs, including hashtags, keywords, and topics.
- Validated and cleaned incoming data for accuracy and relevance before analysis.
- Monitored social media mentions to analyze sentiment, brand perception, and trends.
- Developed Power BI dashboards and analytical reports for brand and competitor performance.

## Relevance to data engineering

This work involved translating reporting needs into data requirements and checking data quality before downstream analysis. It gave me practical experience preparing reliable data for dashboards and business decisions.

**Tool:** Power BI.

**Focus:** Data requirements, validation, cleaning, reporting.
